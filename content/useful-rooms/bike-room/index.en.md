Contact details for the current manager are available on the [Contacts page]({{< ref "/contacts/index.en.md#bike-room" >}}).

For current information, visit our [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/332363472198798/).

The bike room is for storing bicycles belonging to club members who live at the dormitory. It is on the ground floor of block F, next to the entrance.

To be able to access the Bike room you must:

- be a member of the Pod-O-Lee club;
- have active basic membership;
- have a card assigned in Hydra;
- complete the [bike-room member declaration](https://s3.pod.cvut.cz/common/legislativa/prohlaseni_kolarna.pdf);
- email the bike-room manager at <kolarna@pod.cvut.cz>.

## Tool rental

Bike-room members can borrow club tools for basic maintenance and more extensive bicycle repairs. Arrange a loan with the manager or a representative at <kolarna@pod.cvut.cz>.
