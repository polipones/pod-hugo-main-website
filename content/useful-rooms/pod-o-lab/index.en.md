Pod-O-Lab occupies two rooms on the ground floor of block E. The first is primarily a mechanical workshop, while the second is dedicated to electronics and 3D printing.

The lab is open to every club member by prior arrangement. Email the team at [lab@pod.cvut.cz](mailto:lab@pod.cvut.cz) and read the operating rules before visiting.

## Mechanical lab

In the mechanical workshop, you can build or repair almost anything. Equipment includes drill bits for wood and metal, taps, a socket set, an angle grinder, bit sets, crowbars, hammers, saws, and power drills.

A pillar drill, other power tools, files, sheet-metal shears, and compressor make larger jobs easier. Precision tools include marking equipment, a centre punch, and callipers. Cleaning supplies, brushes, rags, gloves, cable ties, adhesive tapes, and protective equipment are also available. Come and visit us; there is almost always a way to take something apart or build it back up.

## Electrical lab

{{<figure src="images/useful-rooms/pod-o-lab/ellab.jpg" alt="Electrical lab" imgop="rt_fit">}}

The second room is equipped for electronics work, with laboratory power supplies, an oscilloscope, signal generator, soldering iron, hot-air station, and other tools. A desktop computer connected to the club network provides the necessary software, including tools for recording data from instruments. We can also help with microcontroller programming. Whether you are building a simple circuit, a school project, or a thesis prototype, the lab provides a suitable workspace.

## 3D printing

{{<figure src="images/useful-rooms/pod-o-lab/3dprint.jpg" alt="3D printing" imgop="rt_fit">}}

The electronics workshop also contains a 3D-printing corner with three printers. A Prusa MK3S and an older dual-extruder Poseidon Duo use FDM technology. A Prusa SL1 resin printer is paired with a Prusa CW1 washing and curing station. Contact the team if you need to print a part or figure; for larger prints, please bring your own filament or resin.
