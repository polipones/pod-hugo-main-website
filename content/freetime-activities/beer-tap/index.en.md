Administrators: [Contacts]({{< ref "/contacts/index.en.md#dormitory-group" >}})

Our beer tap has two faucets and an integrated compressor.

Several types of keg couplers are available:

- “Bayonet”, S-type – the most common coupler, used for Pilsner Urquell, Gambrinus, Radegast, Birell, Kozel, Budvar, Lobkowicz, Primátor, and others
- “Flat”, A-type – used for Svijany, Krakonoš, Staropramen, Litovel, and others
- “Combi”, M-type – similar to the A-type but not interchangeable; used for Kofola and some other beverages

## How to make a reservation

1. Make a reservation in the [reservation system](https://hydra.pod.cvut.cz/reservations/).
2. Email your name, date, ID, and room number to <party@pod.cvut.cz>.
3. The dormitory group leader or a deputy will contact you to arrange collection of the beer tap and accessories.
4. You must pay a **refundable deposit of 2,000 CZK** when collecting the equipment.

Make your reservation **in advance, ideally one week beforehand**, so that we have enough time to prepare the equipment.

## Beer-tap lending rules

1. The person who borrows the beer tap is responsible for it.
2. The dormitory group leader or a deputy lends the beer tap in exchange for the refundable deposit and a dormitory ID.
3. The borrower must report any existing damage immediately after collecting the beer tap.

Before returning the beer tap:

- clean its exterior;
- clean the beer lines and all parts that come into contact with beer; **do not clean the air lines**.

Report any damage that occurs during the loan and agree on compensation with the administrator. Unreported damage will be treated as deliberate damage to club equipment.

**Breaking these rules may result in being barred from borrowing the beer tap, grill, and party room. A serious or repeated violation may result in a proposed three-month suspension of basic Pod-O-Lee membership.**
